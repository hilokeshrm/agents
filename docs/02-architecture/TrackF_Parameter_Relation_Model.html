FIG. 01 CALCULATION CHAIN

Only four parameters touch money

Everything else in the workbook is a dimension, a validator, or narrative. V1, V3, J1 and T1 are the sole inputs to every currency figure — which is exactly why the agent may propose changes to J1 and nothing else. Two parameters are collected on every row and consumed by nothing.

FIG. 02 DIMENSION HIERARCHY

Two roll-up trees over the same row

Every opportunity is indexed twice — once commercially (who buys it) and once by product (what it is). Part# is not a child of either: it cuts across the commercial tree as the aggregation key for supply.

FIG. 03 LIFECYCLE STATE MODEL

Design Status drives confidence — Stage checks it

The funnel runs left to right with a baseline J1 at each state. Stage is a second, independent axis: it should sit directly beneath its status. Anything off the vertical is an internal contradiction, and that is precisely the signal the agent's stage_status_mismatch rule exists to catch.

FIG. 04 CROSS-SHEET JOINS

Nothing joins to anything

The six tabs are meant to describe one pipeline at different resolutions. In practice no two tabs share a key, so every relation below is eyeballed rather than computed. This is the structural blocker — until it's closed, none of the other figures can be automated.

FIG. 05 JUDGMENT LAYER WIRING

Three of six rubric rules cannot fire

Your judgment.py defines six rules. Four have the parameters they need. named_competitor_threat reads a field that exists only on the tab where confidence doesn't, and submitter_calibration reads two fields that don't exist at all.

FIG. 06 AGENT I/O CONTRACT & LLM BOUNDARY

What goes in, where the LLM is allowed, what comes out

Every parameter is read. Only four reach the arithmetic, which is plain Python with no model involvement. Everything the LLM does happens inside the dashed teal boundary — it reasons over evidence and proposes a single number. It cannot alter a computed figure.

FIG. 07 REMEDIATION ORDER

Five changes, in dependency order

These are sequential, not parallel — each one is what makes the next one meaningful. Adding actuals before there's a stable ID gives you numbers you can't attribute to anything.

Reading the seven together

  1. The arithmetic is small and safe. Fig. 01 shows four inputs and two formulas. That's the entire money model — easy to keep auditable, and the reason the agent's write access can be limited to a single field.
  2. The judgment surface is a single number. J1 multiplies revenue and nothing else. Fig. 05 shows six rules all converging on it, and Fig. 06 shows it is the only thing the agent may write.
  3. Seven of ten analyses already run. The catalogue under Fig. 06 separates what today's parameters support from what is genuinely blocked — the blocked ones need fields, not smarter models.
  4. The failures are structural, not analytical. Fig. 04 is the real blocker: no keys, no history, no outcomes. Better modelling on top of unjoined tabs buys nothing.
  5. Two parameters are collected and discarded. Unit/Set and Resale ASP sit on every row and feed no formula. Both are free analysis — attach rate and channel margin — sitting unused in data you already collect.
